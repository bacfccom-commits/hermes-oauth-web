About the Application

Hermes Google Sheets is a personal automation application operated on a private Ubuntu VPS.

Google Data Access

With the account owner's authorization, the application accesses Google Drive and Google Sheets to process files and generate reports.

Data Use

Google data is used for authorized automation tasks, including preparing reports delivered through Telegram.

Data Sharing

Report content may be sent through Telegram to destinations configured by the account owner. Google data is not sold to advertisers.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and any stored data are managed on the private VPS. Access is restricted to authorized operators.

Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner may request deletion of locally stored data and revoke Google access through Google Account settings.
